Concept

Between calm and tension.

Voice, water, and electronic texture drift between calm and tension in a search for moments of connection and clarity.

Written for quadraphonic playback, the composition treats space as part of its musical form. Sounds approach, recede, and pass between four channels, allowing fragile vocal traces and fluid textures to appear briefly before dissolving into the surrounding field.
