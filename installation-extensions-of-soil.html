MIT ACT Gallery
November 7–14, 2025

Concept / Sound Design

Created with Coco Allred and Ophelia Worbes, Extensions of Soil is a walking, interactive installation formed primarily from suspended paper strips. Visitors move through the work as paper, sound, and projected images reshape the space around them.

As sound designer, I expanded the original five-minute composition into a twenty-minute surround-sound work and embedded a five-channel system within the installation. The presentation also brought together sand painting, projection, a live trio music performance, and surround-sound music.
