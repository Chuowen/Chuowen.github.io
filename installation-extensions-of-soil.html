of Soil

MIT ACT Gallery
November 7–14, 2025

Concept / Sound Design

Created with artist Coco Allred and composer Ophelia Ariadne Worbes, the installation uses woven strings and meshes to evoke mycelial networks; as sound designer, I expanded the original five-minute composition into a twenty-minute spatial work and embedded a five-channel multichannel system within the gallery.
