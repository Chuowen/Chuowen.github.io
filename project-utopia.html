Concept

An island imagined through sound.

Based on Wisława Szymborska’s poem, the piece explores an abandoned island through sound—moving from dynamic processing into harmonic contrast while holding a continuous musical stream.

The island becomes less a destination than a shifting state of mind. Layered electronic timbres suggest certainty at a distance, while changes in density, resonance, and harmonic color gradually reveal instability beneath the imagined landscape.
