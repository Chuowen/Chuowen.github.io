01 / Introduction

An experimental
sound practice.

Carolyne (Chuowen) Liang is a composer and sound artist working with electronic music, sound design, and multichannel systems. A graduate of Berklee College of Music’s Electronic Production & Design program, she approaches making as a form of practice-based research. Her work moves between experimental composition, interactive installation, and audiovisual media, using sound to investigate listening, space, perception, and the relationships that emerge between people, technology, and their environments.
